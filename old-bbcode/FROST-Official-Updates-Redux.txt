[size=4][b]What is FROST?[/b][/size]

[size=3]FROST is the successor to the much beloved and much hated [url=http://www.nexusmods.com/newvegas/mods/57927/?]DUST Survival Simulator[/url] for Fallout: New Vegas. 
The goal is to transform the world of Fallout 4 into a freeform survival game with an emphasis on lore and visual storytelling.

Set in the years following the Great War of 2077 during a Nuclear Winter, you will see new lore,
challenging new enemies, and many overhauled gameplay systems.
Many areas in the game world have been visually overhauled as well.
FROST stays true to the Fallout lore and is very lore-friendly.[/size]

[b][size=4]What is the Frost Official Updates mod?[/size][/b]

[size=3]This mod continues the development of FROST. My mod was designed to be used together with Frost Plus and Frost Cell fixes﻿.
It makes FROST more balanced and removes certain exploits, bugs and oversights, and while adding a lot more features.

With over 20.000 edits this mod is one of the largest mods for Fallout 4.
This mod does so many things that it could be split into 30-50 different mods.[/size]

[size=3][b]Q: My game crashes and has bugs, this sucks!
A: [/b]That sounds like [i]you[/i] messed up your load order and mod set-up. Please make sure to follow our
FROST modding guide. We worked very hard to make FROST as stable as
possible, if you follow our modding guide you will not experience any crashes at all.
You can ask us on the FROST Discord for help if you need it, we will help you fix issues with your FROST load order.


[b]Q: Will there be a X-Box or PlayStation version of FROST?
A: [/b]No, because it isn’t possible to port it over properly from a technical perspective.


[b]Q: Do I need all of the DLCs mentioned in the “Requirements Section” from above?
A: [/b]Yes, they are hard requirements. All major DLCs except Creation Club content and the High-Res DLC are required.


[b]Q: Will there be a non-DLC version of FROST?
A: [/b]No, there will not be such a version.


[b]Q: Why do I need the mods mentioned in the guide above just to play FROST?
A:[/b] It isn’t easy to overhaul a game completely with just one plugin/mod.
There are development and technical reasons to keep certain stuff separated.
For example, the Frost Cell Fixes has to be kept separate and at the end of the load order because of how
the engine of Fallout 4 handles performance technologies like preculling.


[b]Q: Any Nexus Collection for FROST?
A: [/b]The Frost Development team explicitly does not endorse or recommend any FROST Nexus Collection as they often have a lot of technical issues and are outdated.

[b]
Q: Any Wabbajack for FROST?
A: [/b]Yes, you can use the unofficial [url=https://github.com/St1ck2k/FROST—-Below-Zero-Fallout-4-Wabbajack-Modlist/tree/main]FROST - Below Zero Fallout 4 Wabbajack Modlist[/url] which implements the Recommended Setup from the Below Zero guide. [b]The old and previously featured Stay FROSTy Wabbajack is not available anymore as of April 24th 2024.[/b][/size]
