

---
## Description

This patches the Wattz Laser Gun (Legacy) mod for FROST
Survivors, Alliance Soldiers, Federation Soldiers and US Army Remnant can also carry the Wattz Rifle.
Unique versions of the Wattz Laser Rifle have been removed or changed.

---
## Technical Details

This patch was made for **version 1.3.1** of the **[Wattz Laser Gun (Legacy)](https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/26386?tab=files)**.
You need to install this patch right after you've installed Wattz Laser Gun (Legacy).
