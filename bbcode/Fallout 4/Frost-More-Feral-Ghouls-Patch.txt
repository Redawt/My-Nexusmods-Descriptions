[size=5][b]Description[/b][/size]

A patch to make the [b][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/43441]More Feral Ghouls[/url][/b] mod compatible with FROST. 
This includes leveled character changes, balance changes, immersion changes and more.
It is compatible with my Frost FGEP Patch.

[line]
[size=5][b]Technical Information[/b][/size]

This patch was made for [b]version 2.0[/b] of the [b][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/43441]More Feral Ghouls[/url][/b] mod.
You should also use the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/59652]Frost Cell Fixes (FCF) patch[/url] for More Feral Ghouls to fix a small issue with precombines.
