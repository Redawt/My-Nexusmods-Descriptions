[size=5][b]Description[/b][/size]

This mod contains two plugins. One changes the [color=#d98f40][b]62 garden gnomes[/b][/color] in Far Harbor. 
They now explode in a [color=#d98f40][b]nuclear fashion[/b][/color] if they get damaged.
I recommend to install & forget this mod, so that you eventually wonder where that random explosion came from
when wandering through Mt. Desert Island.

The other plugin adds a [color=#d98f40][b]Tactical Thermonuclear Gnome grenade[/b][/color] that you can craft at the Chemistry Workbench.

The plugins are esl-flagged esp files, so they don't count towards the plugin limit.

They are also independent from each other, you can enable both or only one of them.
