[size=5][b][b][color=#d98f40][b]Note about the "Next Gen" Update[/b][/color][/b][/b][/size]

FROST only works properly with the [b]downgraded version of Fallout 4[/b], for the following reasons:
[list][*]Many mod authors who made F4SE mods decided to only provide support for the downgraded versions  
[*]The Next Gen version of Fallout 4 has severe bugs, including [b]FPS issues if mods are used that edit many NPCs[/b]. 
[*]The downgraded version has the [b]best compatibility[/b] with many essential mods  
[*]Many mod authors cannot upgrade their F4SE mods properly due to engine-level changes
[/list]
