## Description

This mod adds two kinds of Hygiene Kit with which you can clean yourself without swimming in a source of water.

Hygiene Kits can be crafted at Cooking Pots, and can be used like other consumable items.
Your character will unequip most items for a short time, and then re-equip them.

The scripts in this mod simply trigger a controller quest from **[Get Dirty (take a bath)](https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/29171)**.

---
## Technical Information

This add-on was made for **[GET DIRTY (take a bath)](https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/29171?tab=description) version 2.0**.

Simply load this mod somewhere after the original mod in your load order.
